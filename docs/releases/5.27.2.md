# V5.27.2 — Effective talent gains and equipment health review

Local acceptance date: 2026-11-09. Baseline: `847179d` (V5.27.1).

## Behavior

- The inspected talent shows effective build totals for same-face guidance, critical/follow-up chance, luck, vitality/defense and all 15 deep nodes. Shared probability functions and capped engine stats drive the preview. Other nodes keep their contribution/trigger previews; no universal combat score is introduced.
- Zero effective gain and partial limits are explicit. Investment remains legal, because a different build or later trigger can make a point useful. Current-layer luck limits and overflow gold are shown without promising immediate rewards.
- Equipment comparisons label maximum health explicitly. A swap that clips current health opens a confirmation with exact before/after health, maximum health and the loss. Cancel changes nothing. Uninjured-capacity increases, swaps below the new cap and defeated-state swaps remain direct and never heal.
- Confirmed swaps recheck the complete reviewed state inside the existing save lock, followed by the existing full persisted-snapshot check. Failed primary writes do not equip or consume health. The existing mirror-warning contract is unchanged.

No schema/key changes, new dependencies, automatic actions, balance changes or additional persistence are introduced. Previews are temporary reads; they do not consume RNG or read unrelated optional reserve fields.
